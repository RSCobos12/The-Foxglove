# Email Design Standard

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** Scott Cobos  
**Last Updated:** July 26, 2026

---

# Purpose

The Foxglove email system exists to extend the visual identity and tone of the website into every official communication.

Emails should feel like a natural extension of The Foxglove website rather than automated system messages.

Every email should communicate professionalism, tradition, and understated luxury.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Foxglove emails should be:

- Elegant
- Timeless
- Premium
- Conversational
- Minimal

The objective is to create confidence through simplicity.

Avoid unnecessary graphics, excessive decoration, or marketing language.

Less is more.

---

# Color Palette

## Background

Foxglove Navy

Used as the surrounding canvas for every email.

---

## Content Card

Warm Cream

Provides contrast while maintaining a premium editorial appearance.

---

## Accent Color

Foxglove Gold

Used for:

- Dividers
- Buttons
- Small accents
- Utility links

Gold should never dominate the design.

---

# Header Standard

Every official Foxglove email begins with:

- Foxglove Seal
- Centered gold divider with diamond accent
- Page title

Do not display "The Foxglove" beside the seal.

The seal should speak for itself.

---

# Typography

## Headlines

Georgia

Bold

Centered

---

## Body Copy

Georgia

17px

Generous line spacing

Editorial tone.

---

## Utility Text

Arial

Used only for:

- Button text
- Expiration notices
- Footer utility copy

---

# Content Guidelines

Write naturally.

Avoid corporate language.

Avoid marketing language.

Write as though correspondence is coming from a private tournament committee.

Emails should feel personal without being casual.

---

# Calls To Action

Use a single primary action whenever possible.

Examples:

- Accept Invitation
- Reset Password

Buttons should always use:

- Foxglove Gold background
- Navy text
- Uppercase labels
- Moderate letter spacing

Avoid multiple competing buttons.

---

# White Space

Use generous spacing between sections.

Avoid excessive spacing within paragraphs.

Invitation emails may use more dramatic spacing than account management emails.

Functional emails should prioritize clarity.

---

# Footer Standard

Every official Foxglove email concludes with:

- Gold divider
- Foxglove Seal
- THE FOXGLOVE
- Official Correspondence
- Copyright

Do not include:

- "The Foxglove Invitational"
- Marketing slogans
- Social media icons
- Promotional links

The footer exists only to reinforce brand identity.

---

# Tone By Email Type

## Invitations

Purpose:

Build anticipation.

Language should be warm, welcoming, and aspirational.

---

## Account Management

Examples:

- Password Reset
- Email Verification
- Security Notifications

Purpose:

Create reassurance.

Language should be calm, direct, and concise.

---

## Administrative Notifications

Purpose:

Communicate information efficiently.

Remain conversational while avoiding unnecessary emotion.

---

# Accessibility

Every email should include:

- Alt text for all images
- Sufficient color contrast
- Plain-text fallback link beneath the primary CTA
- Responsive layout
- Readable typography

---

# Approved Email Templates

Current production templates:

- Member Invitation
- Password Recovery

Future templates should follow this standard unless an exception is specifically approved.

---

# Guiding Principle

> Every email should feel like it belongs to The Foxglove before the first sentence is read.
