# The Foxglove Brand Guide

## Brand Identity

The Foxglove is not a golf trip.

It is an annual tradition built around championship golf, fine whiskey, premium cigars, and lifelong brotherhood.

Every design decision should reinforce that identity.

---

# Brand Personality

- Timeless
- Understated
- Refined
- Masculine
- Sophisticated
- Invitational
- Intentional

---

# Design Inspiration

- Augusta National
- The Masters
- Pebble Beach
- Rolex
- Ralph Lauren
- Private Whiskey Clubs

---

# Color Philosophy

Navy represents prestige.

Gold represents legacy.

Cream represents tradition.

Colors should never feel loud.

---

# Typography

Elegant.

Readable.

Never trendy.

Hierarchy should always be clear.

FOXGLOVE should remain the visual centerpiece.

---

# Photography

Whenever possible:

Use authentic Foxglove photography.

Avoid generic stock imagery.

Photography should tell the story of the event.

---

# Motion

Animation should never call attention to itself.

Motion should reward interaction.

Luxury through restraint.

---

# Visual Identity

Primary Mark
- Foxglove Crest

Secondary Mark
- Gold Foxglove Blossom (Future)

Decorative Elements
- Gray Botanical Foxglove Illustrations

---

# Copywriting

Write like an invitation.

Not an advertisement.

Every word should reinforce tradition.

---

# The Foxglove Promise

Before a guest takes their first golf swing, they should already feel that joining The Foxglove means becoming part of a tradition they will want to return to for the rest of their life.

---

# Final Standard

If a design element would feel out of place at Augusta National or in a distinguished private club...

It does not belong at The Foxglove.
