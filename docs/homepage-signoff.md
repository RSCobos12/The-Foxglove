# Project Vision

The objective of the homepage was never simply to create a golf tournament website.

Its purpose was to establish the identity of The Foxglove.

Visitors should immediately experience:

- Prestige
- Tradition
- Brotherhood
- Competition
- Exclusivity
- Monterey
- Sophistication

before reading a single paragraph.

The homepage serves as the front entrance to The Foxglove.

# Design Philosophy

Throughout development, every design decision followed one principle:

**When choosing between two options, choose the quieter one.**

The homepage intentionally avoids unnecessary decoration.

Luxury is communicated through confidence and restraint.

---

# Final Assessment

The homepage successfully communicates:

- Exclusivity
- Tradition
- Premium craftsmanship
- Invitational atmosphere

without relying on excessive visual effects.

The experience feels intentional, cinematic, and refined.

The homepage establishes the design standard for every page that follows.
