# The Foxglove Design Principles

## Core Identity

The Foxglove should feel like:

- Masters-level prestige
- Private whiskey and cigar club atmosphere
- Coastal golf tradition
- Brotherhood, ceremony, competition, and memory

## Guiding Standard

Every design decision should answer:

> Does this feel like an invitation to an exclusive golf tradition?

## Principles

- Luxury through restraint.
- Reward attention. Never demand it.
- Every animation must have a purpose.
- Every word should reinforce tradition.
- Photography should feel authentic whenever possible.
- The site should feel timeless, not trendy.
- The brand should feel confident, not loud.
- The homepage must be finalized before building interior pages.

## Things We Avoid

- Loud gradients
- Neon colors
- Busy backgrounds
- Cartoon icons
- Over-explaining
- Generic event language
- Cheap-looking buttons
- Excessive animation
- Random stock photography when real photography is available

## Homepage Final Requirements

- Replace static hero image with looping hero video.
- Replace Welcome botanical placeholders with gray foxglove illustrations.
- Replace card Roman numerals with small gold foxglove icons when final card photos are added.
- Replace placeholder card images with curated Foxglove photography.
