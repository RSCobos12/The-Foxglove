Welcome to The Foxglove

This is not simply a tournament.
It is a tradition—an invitation to step away from the noise
and into something enduring.

Here, we gather as brothers and competitors.
We drink fine whiskey. We smoke good cigars.
We play quality golf on storied grounds.
And in doing so, we build memories that last a lifetime.

The Foxglove is a celebration of the bonds we share,
the rivalries that sharpen us, and the moments
that remind us why we play.

Welcome, gentlemen.
Let the games begin.
