Welcome to The Foxglove

Every tradition begins with a single gathering.
Ours is built on spirited golf, fine beverages, a touch of tobacco,
and the kind of brotherhood that only a little relentless trash talk can produce.

Like the foxglove flower itself, there is a touch of poison here.
Beautiful at first glance, yet carrying just enough toxicity to keep
every wager personal, every birdie celebrated a little too loudly,
and every missed putt remembered far longer than it should be.
It's all in good fun- and perhaps a mild personality flaw or two.

Leave your excuses behind. Bring your best game, your sharpest
wit, and the courage to back up every word.

Play to win. Chirp ruthlessly. Shake hands afterwards.
Drink fine whiskey. Smoke quality cigars. Swing your swing.

Welcome, gentlemen.
The challenge is simple: Earn the stories.

— The Foxglove
