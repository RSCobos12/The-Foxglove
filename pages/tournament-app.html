The Foxglove

Tournament App

The Official App for The Foxglove Invitational

The Foxglove Tournament App is the official live scoring platform used by members throughout the tournament.

Stay connected from the first tee through the final putt with live standings, accurate digital scorecards, round information and your personal Foxglove player profile—all directly from your phone.

Powerful Features. Built for the Game.

Everything you need on tournament day.

01

Home Screen

Your tournament at a glance.

The home screen gives members an immediate snapshot of the tournament whenever they open the app.

A preview of the current live leaderboard keeps you connected to the competition while the round card displays the date of the upcoming round, your scheduled tee time and current weather conditions in the area.

  • Live leaderboard preview
  • Upcoming round date
  • Member tee time
  • Local weather conditions
02

Live Leaderboard

Follow the tournament in real time.

The live leaderboard shows the current tournament standings as scores are submitted by groups throughout the course.

Members can quickly see player position, current score and progress through the round, making it easy to know where everyone stands.

  • Real-time tournament standings
  • Current player position
  • Score and holes completed
  • Overall and individual round views
03

Digital Scorecard

Accurate scoring for the course you’re playing.

Each scorecard accurately reflects the course and tournament round being played, giving members the same hole, par and scoring information they would expect from a traditional scorecard.

Scores are entered quickly using the thumb scroller. Members can move from player to player, allowing one golfer to serve as the official scorer for the entire group—or allowing each player to keep their own individual score.

  • Course-specific scorecards
  • Round-specific hole information
  • Easy thumb-scroller score entry
  • Score one player or the entire group
04

Player Profile

Your Foxglove record in one place.

Every member has a personal profile containing their player information and current handicap index.

As tournaments are completed, the profile becomes a permanent record of each member’s Foxglove history, including previous finishes and scoring results.

  • Personal member profile
  • Current handicap index
  • Previous tournament finishes
  • Foxglove scoring history

Add It To Your Phone

Install The Foxglove App

The Foxglove Tournament App is a Progressive Web App, so there is no traditional App Store download. Open the app in your browser and add it directly to your phone’s home screen.



iPhone

iOS

  1. 1
    Open Safari

    Open the Foxglove Tournament App link using Safari on your iPhone.

  2. 2
    Tap Share

    Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari.

  3. 3
    Add to Home Screen

    Scroll through the Share menu and select “Add to Home Screen.”

  4. 4
    Tap Add

    Confirm the name and tap Add. The Foxglove icon will appear on your home screen.

A

Android

Chrome

  1. 1
    Open Chrome

    Open the Foxglove Tournament App link using Chrome on your Android phone.

  2. 2
    Open the Menu

    Tap the three-dot Chrome menu in the upper-right corner.

  3. 3
    Install the App

    Select “Install app” or “Add to Home screen,” depending on your version of Chrome.

  4. 4
    Confirm

    Confirm the installation and the Foxglove icon will be added to your home screen.
